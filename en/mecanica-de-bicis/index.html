A hands-on workshop to stop being afraid of your bike. You bring your own bike, we put it on the stand and, step by step, you learn to fix what breaks most often: mend a puncture, adjust the brakes, tune the gears, clean and lube the chain and do a basic check before you ride.

No experience or tools needed: we provide the bench, the stand and the tools, and the teacher shows you how to use them on your own bike. You leave knowing what to check and how to fix it at home.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to bring my own bike?+

Yes: the workshop is done on your bike, so you learn exactly what it needs. If you are unsure about your bike, write to us before booking.

Do I need experience or tools?+

No. We provide the stand and the tools and start from scratch.

What if my bike needs a new part?+

Spare parts (inner tubes, cables, brake pads) are not included. If you know what is wrong, bring them; if not, we tell you which one to buy.

Can I keep working on my bike another day?+

Yes: rent a workbench by the hour and carry on at your own pace, with the tools at hand.
