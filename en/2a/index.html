What is Taller Benac?

Taller Benac is a space in Valencia, in Camins al Grau, to make things with your hands and have fun doing it. You can book mosaic, paint and wine, ceramics and bike mechanics, where you bring your own bike and learn to fix it. Very soon we will open woodworking, laser engraving, knife making and leather.

If you already know what you want to make, rent one of our five workbenches with tools, in the daytime from 10:00 to 17:00: to fix your bike, make a knife or build your own project. Come alone, as a couple or with friends: you bring the curiosity; we bring the space, the tools and someone who knows.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?+

No. The workshops are designed for beginners, with someone who knows by your side.

Can I bring my own bike?+

Yes, that is the idea of the bike mechanics workshop: you bring your bike and learn to fix it yourself.

How much does it cost to rent a workbench?+

€10 an hour for one person and €2 more for each extra person, up to 6 people per workbench (€20 an hour). It is hired in the daytime, from 10:00 to 17:00, also while a workshop is on, and we have five workbenches.

What about the workshops without dates yet?+

Tap Notify me on their card and we will write when there is a date. Once enough people are interested, we run the first session.

Are you a group or a company?+

We run private sessions of the activities that already have dates. Ask for a quote on the Groups page.

Where are you?+

In Valencia, in the Camins al Grau neighbourhood. We open at 10:00; workshops run in the afternoons and at weekends. The address, opening hours and directions are at the bottom of the page.
