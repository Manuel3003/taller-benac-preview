What you can do at your workbench

A workbench is your own bench for the hours you book: power and hand tools, room for up to 6 people and someone from the team nearby if you get stuck. You bring the project and the materials. Some ideas:

  • Fix your bike

    Change an inner tube, adjust brakes and gears or lube the chain, with the tools at hand.

  • Make a knife

    Sand, polish and fit the handle of your knife, or sharpen the ones you have.

  • Build a shelf

    Measure, cut, drill and screw together your own made-to-measure piece.

  • Restore furniture

    Sand, repair and refinish that chair or chest of drawers you want to bring back.

Bring your materials (wood, parts, spares); we provide the space and the tools. Questions about your project? Write to us before booking.

Rules and safety protocol

You have a project and no space or tools. Book a workbench by the hour in Valencia (Camins al Grau): a sturdy bench, power and hand tools, and someone from the team nearby if you get stuck.

Good for a one-off repair or for working on your project every week. And if you bring friends, the bench fits up to six people.

Workbench rules

  1. A safety talk before using any machine
  2. Bring your materials: wood, parts and consumables
  3. Leave the bench clean and the tools where they belong
  4. No minors on machines
  5. Everyone at the bench counts towards the maximum of six

Safety protocol

Before you switch on any machine we walk you through the workshop safety protocol and how each tool is used. Safety glasses and ear protection included.

If you have never used a tool, tell us when you book and we will help you get started.

Frequently asked questions

When can I rent a workbench?+

In the daytime, from 10:00 to 17:00, also while a workshop is on. We have five workbenches: book ahead if you come as a group.

Can I leave my project half-finished?+

Ask us when you book: it depends on the space left in the workshop.

Is there someone to help me?+

There is always someone from the team around. It is not a class, but we help if you get stuck.

I am a pro, can I come often?+

Of course. If you need the bench regularly, write to us and we will talk about hour packs.
